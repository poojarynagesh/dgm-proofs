How each proof is laid out
  1. Statement: what is proved, which lecture, and where it is used later.
  2. Picture: an SVG diagram of the mechanism (the thing to sketch in the exam).
  3. Steps: one line of algebra per step with a tag for the reason (Jensen, Bayes rule, ...).
  4. Numeric check: small concrete numbers that confirm the result.
  5. Exam box and common mistake: what the professor stressed and the usual slip.
How to read the "filled in" labels

Where the lecture only stated a result or skipped algebra, the page derives the missing step and labels it step filled in — not written out in class. Where the page corrects the lecture notes (for example a sign, a constant, or which network is updated 5:1), it says so on the page. Check those against your own notes before the exam.
